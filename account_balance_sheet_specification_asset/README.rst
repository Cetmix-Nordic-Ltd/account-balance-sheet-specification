Itemizes the fixed asset accounts of the asset profiles of
``account_asset_management`` in the balance sheet specification: one
item per asset, its acquisition less the depreciation booked by the
date, taken from the journal items linked to the asset.

Postings on the account without an asset stay unexplained, so they are
easy to spot.

Usage
=====

Nothing to do: compute the specification and the asset accounts are
itemized per asset.
