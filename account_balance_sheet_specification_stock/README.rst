Itemizes the stock valuation accounts of the product categories in the
balance sheet specification: one item per product category with the
stock value on the date, from the stock valuation layers created by the
end of the day.

A difference between the account and the stock value shows inventory
postings still missing from the books, for example with periodic
(manual) valuation.

Usage
=====

Nothing to do: compute the specification and the stock valuation
accounts are itemized per product category.
